Click a block to paint with it, or drag to pick a bigger piece (like a whole house). On the map, use Copy blocks to grab blocks you've already placed.

Block

A block is 4 small tiles, twice: bottom layer · top layer · how it looks. Click a quarter, then click a tile below to put it there.
